Undefeated seasons aren't built on star power. They're built on the boring stuff: hitting the cutoff man, moving the runner over, throwing strike one.

This program has made an art of the boring stuff. The result is a spring without a loss, and a clubhouse that refuses to talk about it.

"We don't say the word," one senior admitted with a grin. "We just keep showing up."
